## Changing the photo

Click **Change photo** in the portrait area and choose a JPG, PNG, or WebP image (up to 10 MB). The photo is resized and saved only in that browser. It is not uploaded or published.

To show your photo to everyone, add your image to `assets` (for example `assets/profile.jpg`) and change the `src` of the `profile-photo` image in `index.html` to that path. Update its `alt` text to `Profile photo of Glaisa Mae Curato`. A previously selected browser-local photo overrides this default; clear the site's browser storage to see the default again.
